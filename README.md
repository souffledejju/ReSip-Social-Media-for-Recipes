# ReSip - Social Media for Recipes 🍳📱

ReSip is an Android-based social media application designed specifically for cooking enthusiasts and culinary lovers. The platform serves as an all-in-one solution to help users discover recipe inspiration, follow visual cooking guides, monitor nutritional intake transparently, and bookmark favorite recipes in one place.

This project was developed collaboratively as an academic requirement.

---

## 📌 Problem Background

Many people face practical challenges when preparing meals at home:
* **Visual Constraints:** Text-heavy recipes often confuse beginners and increase the chance of cooking mistakes due to the lack of clear step-by-step visual guidance.
* **Nutritional & Health Tracking:** Accurately calculating and monitoring daily caloric intake from homemade meals is difficult without built-in nutritional insights.
* **App Fragmentation & Inefficiency:** Users are forced to juggle multiple applications—video streaming platforms for tutorials, web browsers for ingredient lists, and separate health apps for calorie tracking—making the cooking process cumbersome.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Simplify the culinary learning curve through interactive, easy-to-follow in-app video tutorials.
2. Streamline kitchen preparation by offering structured, detailed ingredient checklists for every recipe.
3. Promote healthier lifestyle choices through transparent nutritional data and calorie estimates per serving.

---

## 💡 Key Benefits

* **Reduced Cooking Errors:** Step-by-step visual guides minimize mistakes during meal preparation.
* **Organized Kitchen Prep:** Digital checklists ensure no ingredients or seasonings are overlooked before cooking begins.
* **Centralized Experience:** Integrates tutorials, measurements, timers, and calorie metrics into a single platform without needing external apps.
* **Effortless Calorie Management:** Helps users maintain healthy eating habits with clear per-serving calorie information.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **In-App Video Player:**
  Integrated video playback allowing users to watch sequential cooking steps directly within the app.

* **Interactive Ingredients Checklist:**
  Complete ingredient breakdown with precise measurements (grams, milliliters, units) and checkable items to track preparation progress.

* **Automatic Calorie Calculator:**
  Estimates and displays total calories per recipe as well as calories per serving based on the built-in ingredient catalog.

* **Cooking Time Estimation:**
  Provides estimated prep and cooking duration to assist users in managing their kitchen workflow.

* **Recipe Bookmarking:**
  Enables users to save favorite recipes to a personal collection for quick and easy future access.

* **Recipe Content Management System (CMS):**
  Dedicated interface and workflow for contributors to publish tutorials, format ingredient lists, and manage nutritional metrics.

* **User Profile & Personalization:**
  Stores and manages user identity details, including display names and profile avatars.
